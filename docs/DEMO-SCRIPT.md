# Demo video script. Target 2:45, hard cap 3:00.

Every beat is mapped to a judging criterion: [I] Potential Impact, [W] WebMCP Leverage, [E] Execution, [C] Creativity.

## Setup before recording

- Open https://fairground-umber.vercel.app in ChatGPT's in-app browser, agent panel visible beside the page. Confirm the badge says "WebMCP active".
- Clean profile, no old cases. Rehearse once fully. Record in short clips; you will stitch them.
- Mic on, quiet room, no music (rules forbid copyrighted audio). Speak slowly; short sentences land better than fast ones.
- 1080p or higher. Cursor visible.

## Scene 1: the problem, over a working product (0:00 to 0:20) [I]

ACTION: Screen starts on the landing page. As you speak, scroll gently past the Docket line, then click the demo card "The withheld deposit". You are inside a live case by second 15.

VOICE:
"Maya's landlord kept her 1,200 dollar deposit, even though the move-out checklist was signed clean. A lawyer costs more than the deposit. Small claims court takes months. Ninety-two percent of people in her position get no legal help at all. So they give up. This is Fairground: a neutral courtroom where both sides bring their own AI advocate."

## Scene 2: the agent uses the page (0:20 to 0:55) [W]

ACTION: The case room is open; the landlord's dispute is already filed. In the agent panel, type or say: "Review the claim against the landlord and give me a private reality check. Then set my floor at 800 dollars." Point at the "Your agent on this page" feed as entries appear.

VOICE:
"I just talk. My agent is using this page's WebMCP tools: reviewing the claim, running a private reality check, sealing my bottom line. Watch the feed: every move it makes in my name is visible to me. And here is the core idea. The page decides which tools exist, based on who I am and where the case stands. My agent cannot see the landlord's numbers. It cannot skip a step. And there is no signing tool at all. The procedure is the tool surface."

## Scene 3: Autopilot, sealed offers, mediation (0:55 to 1:45) [W][C][E]

ACTION: The Autopilot bar is now visible (floor is set). Click "Let the agents negotiate". Let rounds fire. Point at the sealed envelopes and the gap signals as they appear. If it goes to mediation, show the proposal card and click Accept.

VOICE:
"Now both advocates negotiate on their own, bounded by my limit. Sealed offers, three rounds. If the two numbers ever overlap, the case settles instantly at the midpoint. If not, the only thing either side learns is whether the gap narrowed. Neither agent can concede past its human's limit. If mine ever wants to, the platform raises an approval card that only I can click. No overlap here, so a neutral mediator, the only party who can see the sealed history, puts one fair number on the table. Both sides accept."

## Scene 4: the human signature (1:45 to 2:15) [C][E]

ACTION: Click "Draft the agreement". Scroll the plain-language agreement briefly. Type your name, click "Sign agreement". The counterpart countersigns; the RESOLVED stamp lands. Point at the record seal at the bottom.

VOICE:
"The agreement is drafted in plain language. And here is the line we drew on purpose: my agent got me here, but it cannot sign. Consent is mine. I sign, the other side signs, resolved. And this seal at the bottom is a cryptographic fingerprint of the signed record. Anyone holding this document can prove, forever, that it was never altered."

## Scene 5: proof and close (2:15 to 2:45) [I][E]

ACTION: Return to the landing page. Point at the live Docket line as you speak. End on the hero headline.

VOICE:
"Does it work? We measured it. Thirty randomized disputes run end to end through this live deployment: one hundred percent settled, at seventy percent of the claim, in under a minute each. Every settlement feeds this public docket, including the one you just watched. Twenty-three WebMCP tools, two opposing agents, one neutral page, and a signature only a human can give. Justice, for disputes too small for lawyers. Fairground."

## If something goes sideways while recording

- Autopilot settles by overlap instead of mediation: skip the mediator lines and say "the offers overlapped, so it settled instantly at the midpoint." Shorter is fine.
- Agent phrasing is off in Scene 2: retry the clip; agents vary run to run. The feed appearing is what matters.
- Total runtime over 2:55: cut Scene 2's last two sentences first; the "no signing tool" line must survive.
