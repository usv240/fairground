# Demo video script. Final cut. Narration ~290 words, lands at 2:30 to 2:45.

Beats map to judging criteria: [I] Impact, [W] WebMCP Leverage, [E] Execution, [C] Creativity.

The deposit demo is now deterministic: it always runs three sealed rounds, then mediation, then acceptance. Rehearse once and the recording will match the rehearsal.

## Recording checklist (from review feedback)

- ChatGPT's in-app browser, agent panel beside the page. The "WebMCP active" badge must be visible in the first agent shot.
- Browser zoom 110 to 125 percent so labels survive YouTube compression.
- Record in short clips and stitch. This is allowed and encouraged: the rules require "a clear demo of your project functioning"; Devpost's own tips say to use jump cuts and trim dead air. No rule requires a continuous take.
- Pause 1 to 2 seconds after the agent feed updates so a judge can read one entry.
- When the floor is set, hold on the line "Private mandate on file: floor $800... the other side can never see it."
- At signing, point the cursor at the on-screen text "Signature: humans only. Fairground exposes no signing tool." That sentence on screen is your proof.
- End on the agreement sheet where amount, both signatures, and the seal are visible together.
- No music. Add YouTube captions after upload (auto-captions, then fix "WebMCP" and "sealed offers").
- Muted test: a judge watching with no sound should still see claim, private limit, sealed rounds, mediation, human signatures, resolved. Every scroll should reveal evidence; cut any scroll that does not.

## Scene 1: the problem, over a working product (0:00 to 0:20) [I]

ACTION: Start on the landing page. While speaking, click the demo card "The withheld deposit". Inside a live case by second 15.

VOICE:
"Maya's landlord kept her twelve hundred dollar deposit, even though she passed the move-out inspection. A lawyer costs more than the deposit. Small claims court takes months. Ninety-two percent of people in her position get no legal help. So most give up. This is Fairground: a neutral resolution room where both sides bring their own AI advocate."

## Scene 2: the agent uses the page (0:20 to 0:55) [W]

ACTION: The landlord's dispute is already filed. In the agent panel, say: "Review the claim and give me a private reality check. Then set my floor at 800 dollars." Let the feed update; pause on it. Then hold on the mandate line showing the floor is private.

VOICE:
"I just talk. My agent uses this page's WebMCP tools: reviewing the claim, running a private reality check, sealing my bottom line at eight hundred dollars. Only I can see that number. And here is the core idea: the page decides which tools exist, based on who I am and where the case stands. There is no signing tool at all. The procedure is the tool surface."

## Scene 3: sealed negotiation and mediation (0:55 to 1:40) [W][C]

ACTION: Click "Let the agents negotiate". Let the three rounds fire; the envelopes and gap signals carry the visuals. The mediator card appears; note the on-screen label "controlled by neither party". Click Accept.

VOICE:
"Both advocates negotiate within limits privately set by their humans. The offers stay sealed. If they overlap, the case settles at the midpoint. If not, each side learns only whether the gap is closing. No overlap here, so a neutral mediator reviews the sealed history and proposes one fair number. Both sides accept."

## Scene 4: the human signature and Maya's result (1:40 to 2:15) [C][E]

ACTION: Click "Draft the agreement". Point at "Signature: humans only". Type the name, sign. Counterpart countersigns; RESOLVED stamp lands. End framed on amount, signatures, and seal together. Read the actual settled amount from the screen.

VOICE:
"The agreement is drafted in plain language. My agent got me here, but it cannot sign. Consent is mine. Both sides sign. Resolved. Maya's dispute is settled: [read the amount on screen] recovered, with a signed, verifiable agreement, and no lawyer. This seal is a fingerprint of the signed record; anyone holding the document can use it to verify the record was never altered."

## Scene 5: proof and close (2:15 to 2:40) [I][E]

ACTION: Return to the landing page. Point at the Docket line. End on the hero headline.

VOICE:
"In product testing, thirty simulated disputes ran end to end through this live deployment. All settled, in under a minute each. Every settlement feeds this docket as anonymous totals, including the one you just watched. Two opposing agents. One neutral page. And a signature only a human can give. Justice, for disputes too small for lawyers. Fairground."
